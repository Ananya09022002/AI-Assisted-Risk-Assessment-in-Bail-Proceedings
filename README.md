# AI-Assisted-Risk-Assessment-in-Bail-Proceedings
Research Paper: An Explainable Analysis of Post-Bail Misconduct in India

Artificial intelligence is increasingly being examined as a decision-support tool within the criminal justice system. However, its application to bail decision-making raises important concerns regarding individual liberty, fairness, transparency and explainability, as well as the preservation of judicial discretion. This study proposes an explainable case-control framework to examine whether pre-bail legal and factual characteristics are associated with documented post-bail misconduct in Indian criminal cases. The study will compare accused persons who were granted bail and subsequently showed documented adverse outcomes, such as failure to appear, absconding, bail-condition violations, witness interference, bail cancellation for breach, or registration of a new case, with comparable granted-bail cases having adequate follow-up and no documented misconduct. Variables such as prior criminal cases, offence category, bail type, charge-sheet status, witness-risk concerns, and absconding-risk concerns will be analysed using logistic regression and related statistical techniques in R. The study will additionally examine model accuracy, explainability, false-positive and false-negative errors, and potential bias across relevant subgroups. The proposed framework  evaluates whether structured, transparent, and contestable AI-assisted risk assessment can support judicial reasoning while preserving human oversight and constitutional safeguards.
Keywords: Artificial Intelligence, Bail, Post-Bail Misconduct, Explainable AI, Criminal Justice
